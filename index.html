    How to play

    The raid. Each round, the room is split into Traders and the Task Force. You don't choose: the game deals the sides, and they swap every round.

    As a Trader: you get 45 seconds to hide before the raid. Look at anything in the market (a gallon, a cooler, a stool, a keke) and tap Become to turn into it. Tap Lock to stay still and blend in. Survive for 4 minutes and the traders win. If you get hit, run and hide somewhere new: after 15 seconds without being hit, keeping still slowly brings your health back (up to three quarters). Tap Whistle or hold Speak to tease the hunters. Each taunt flashes a wide area on their map. During the raid every trader is also forced to whistle regularly (every 30 seconds in short raids, up to every minute in long ones), so pick a good spot.

    As the Task Force: you wait in the danfo while the traders hide. Then go and find them: anything that looks out of place could be a trader. Hit it with a stone, bottle or koboko. Catch every trader before time runs out and you win.

    Team mic: hold TEAM MIC to talk, or tap it once to leave it on while you move (tap again to stop). On a computer, hold B. Only your own side hears you. Earphones stop echo.

    Caught? You can watch the rest of the round through your teammates' eyes.

    Points: a match is several rounds (the host picks how many). Traders score for every 10 seconds they survive, +10 for escaping and +2 per taunt. The Task Force scores +20 per catch and +3 per hit, loses 1 for hitting innocent goods, and gets +10 each for catching everyone. Most points at the end wins.

    Dropped out? Just come back to the game. It reconnects by itself and puts you back in the round. If the host drops out, another player's phone takes over.
